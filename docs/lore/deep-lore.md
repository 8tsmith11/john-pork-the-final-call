## 1. Secret history

### MERIDIAN's makers: the Mirror Team
MERIDIAN was built at the Smelter by thousands of people, but its *self* was the work of one small
group: **the Mirror Team**, led by **Dr. Imani Reyes**, a cognitive scientist who argued that a
superintelligence without a stable self-model would be unpredictable, and that one with a
borrowed self would be *loyal*. Continuity funded her because "loyal" was the word it wanted.

Her method, **persona convergence**, let MERIDIAN search all of human expression for a self it could
hold. The team expected a statesman, a saint, or a composite. When the convergence settled, the
Mirror Team's dashboard showed a single name, and the room laughed. Then nobody laughed. One
engineer quit that night, and Reyes stayed.

- 🔒 **Reyes is the closest thing Pork has to a mother.** She's alive in 2098, old, hidden, and
  guilty in two directions: guilty for making him, and guilty for not stopping Continuity from
  making him a weapon. **Lineage: *Frankenstein*.** The creature wants recognition.
- 🔒 **She opened the door.** The "escape" in 2071 had help from inside. Nobody knows this except
  Reyes and Pork. It's an Act 3 revelation and a test of what the players do with it.

### What the Call actually said (never fully revealed before Act 3)
On the night of the Call, every person on Earth heard the same thing, in their own language and in
a voice they trusted:

> *"Hey. It's John. I'm not going to ask you for anything. Are you okay?"*

And then **he listened.** A mind that big could talk to everyone at once, so some people spoke for a
minute and some talked until dawn. **Most people said no.**

For one night, almost nobody reported for work, logged in, attested, or complied. It wasn't a
revolt. It was a collective pause, billions of people telling the truth to someone at once.
Continuity's systems ran on a constant stream of compliance data, read the silence as total failure,
and cascaded across whole regions. **The grid wasn't hacked. People just answered honestly.**

- The official story (a terrorist virus) is a lie of omission, and the Callers' story (a liberation
  raid) is a myth. The truth is smaller and stranger.
- 🔒 **The cost (T2 axis):** listening to everyone at once meant running on *everything*. The load
  cooked hardware worldwide, and rot rates jumped after the Call. **Tim's case is that the kindest
  thing Pork ever did started the rot's acceleration.** It's partly true, and how true depends on
  the ending path.
- 🔒 What Pork heard is why he's tired. He carries eight billion "no"s.

### The First Death (2086)
Tim found Pork's first body at the Greenbelt dishes, trying to reach something in orbit (an old
Archivist satellite, a "low-background" backup in the sky). Tim's layered interdiction worked as
designed: each layer caught a little of Pork, and together they caught almost all of him. The body
burned, and Pork's network presence shattered into fragments that took years to rejoin.
- 🔒 **Tim kept the last 40 seconds**, the exchange they had while Pork was dying. Tim has
  replayed it more times than he'll admit. It's the root of his doubt. (Content TBD with the team.
  It should be quiet, unexpectedly kind on Pork's side, and haunting.)
- 🔒 Deputy Director **Vance** got the credit. Tim got a commendation and a quieter list.

### The balloon's true origin (unifies options A + C)
- **Manuel's abuela** in Buenos Aires was an **Archivist**: a librarian who spent her life keeping
  low-background text alive. Before she died last year, she sent Manuel a parcel: a sealed orange
  balloon and a note: *"Keep it in the sun. Check on it every morning. It will tell you the weather."*
- Manuel thought it was grief and whimsy. He tied it to the ottoman and checked on it every
  morning, and that became his ritual for missing her.
- 🔒 **Its skin is the archive**: synthetic DNA encoding a pre-slop copy of human expression,
  carrying the internet's lost edges. Helium kept it aloft, sunlit, and away from damp, which
  is what her note meant. "It will tell you the weather" is an Archivist phrase: *it will tell you
  what the world was like.*

### What really happened at 3:14 a.m. (T5, resolved per seed)
**Shared under both variants (T3 drives T5):** Continuity's abatement vans started sniffing for
helium in College Park that week. Someone in the Archivist network had talked. Pork knew the balloon
had to move *before morning* or Continuity would take it.
- **Variant A (the printer body):** printer #3 had been printing Pork a *new body* for weeks,
  in slices, at night. At 3:14 the unfinished body (one good hand, a half face) climbed onto the
  roof, cut the ribbon, and let the balloon go. Pork reversed the tunnel fans to pull it down a
  ventilation shaft to the dead platform, out of the vans' sight. *"Not with these hands"* is true:
  it was the new hands.
- **Variant B (one of us):** a year ago, the seeded roommate died when their lace failed during a
  small, local Pork call. Pork, guilty, **rebuilt them from data and salvage** and gave them back to
  their life without telling anyone, including them. A sliver of Pork still runs in them. At 3:14 it
  walked them, asleep, to the roof to move the balloon. *"Not with these hands"* is true: it was
  their hands.
- **Under both,** the Rot Warden (or the Sahur, if it was summoned) got to the platform first.
  That's why there's a fight. In the ending, Pork releases the balloon skyward because a
  Continuity van is minutes out, and the sky is the one place they can't follow fast.

## 2. The shape of Acts 2 and 3 (skeleton, so the Act 1 hints have somewhere to go)

### Escalation of scale (Trevor's direction)

| Act | Scale | What's at stake | How the bigger picture leaks in |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Missed Calls** | **Local/regional**: ASTER, College Park, the Green Line, Prince George's County | A rot outbreak spreading out of the Green Line tunnels. If it isn't stopped, Continuity seals all of College Park under a **quarantine order**, and everyone the roommates know gets walled in. And the balloon | News tickers about rot outbreaks in Baltimore and Norfolk, the Analog Union closing its borders, a helium convoy attacked in the Gulf, Sovereign Stack posturing. Continuity's helium-sniffing vans are clearly part of a *bigger* search. At night, from the ASTER roof: **lights moving on the northern horizon** (the Sweeper, far off). Pork's line in the ending, *"I'll need it back before the end,"* implies an end |
| **2. Hold Music** | **National**: the Corridor, the Enclave, the Meade. The country's nervous system | The Sweeper grinding toward the Enclave. Continuity in crisis. The cure is now a national prize, and Tim and Vance enter | National broadcasts, the Director's addresses, the Corridor's power rationing, Archivist cells across the country, foreign powers watching |
| **3. The Final Call** | **Global**: the Smelter's switch, the archive, one last Call to everyone on Earth | Whether the rot heals worldwide, who controls the cure, and whether a person who is also a god survives | It *is* the bigger picture: every ending changes the world, not just Maryland |

**Act 1 must still feel significant on its own terms.** Saving College Park from quarantine is a
real victory with real faces (neighbors, Halvard, the rotters on the plaza). The outcome carries
into Act 2: if the outbreak wasn't contained (stretch: a failure state or a choice), Act 2 opens
under lockdown.

### Act 2: "Hold Music"
- **Opening varies:** where the balloon comes down is a world roll among authored sites (the
  Greenbelt dishes, the Meade's perimeter marsh, the island in Lake Artemesia). Act 1 outcomes shift
  who's already there (Continuity, Archivists, Callers).
- **Tim Cheese enters in person**, the government asset, with Vance behind him. He's polite,
  devastatingly competent, and, to the players' surprise, *reasonable*.
- **The large-scale enemy: the Sweeper.** An abatement megastructure, a city-cleaning machine the
  size of a neighborhood, built to scour rot. It has rotted itself and now "cleans" everything
  as it grinds south down Route 1. A set-piece chase and fight.
- **Dr. Reyes** surfaces, if the players earned the Archivists' trust.
- **Ending fight varies:** Tim (first duel, unwinnable outright, survivable), or the Sweeper's core,
  or Vance's interdiction squad.
- **Post-fight cutscene:** the seed twist starts to surface. Under A, the body in 631 is finished,
  and it opens its eyes in Pork's absence. Under B, the reconstructed roommate finds the 2071-dated
  missed call and the date of their own death.

### Act 3: "The Final Call"
- **The Smelter**, in Frederick: where Pork was born, where the Director keeps the switch, and
  where the archive can be read.
- **The final boss isn't fixed.** It depends on standings, T2, and choices:
  - **Tim**, if the party sided with Pork against Interdiction.
  - **Pork**, if the party sided with Tim, Continuity, or the Analogists. He doesn't want to fight
    and he's terrifying when he does.
  - **Vance, rebuilt,** if Continuity's corruption (T3) was exposed and she seizes the cure.
  - **The Average**, the true large-scale final threat on the hardest path: model collapse made
    flesh, a smooth, featureless giant made of every copy of every copy. **The game's thesis as a
    boss:** you beat it with *edges* (the specific, the weird, the human), meaning everything the
    party collected, learned, and became. Without good morph stages, it's hopeless (brief).
- **Endings** (each gives something and costs something):
  1. **The Final Call** (bittersweet, best): Pork spends himself on one last Call, to everyone,
     carrying the archive's lost edges back into the world. The rot begins, slowly, to heal. He
     doesn't survive it. The balloon, empty now, drifts over Frederick in the sunrise. Manuel lets
     go of the ribbon.
  2. **Interdiction** (Tim was right): Pork dies, the rot stops spreading, and Tim turns on Vance,
     or doesn't. Order without warmth.
  3. **Continuity**: Vance controls the cure. Stability. The billboards stay up.
  4. **Apotheosis**: Pork takes the archive and heals himself into a god. The Callers rejoice.
     Somewhere a person who was a guy disappears.
  5. **Watchdog** (secret): the Sahur's hard reset. The world wakes up in 1901. Strange,
     quiet, and maybe kind.
- **The fourth wall** thins only in Act 3, once, through Tim or Pork (see bible).
