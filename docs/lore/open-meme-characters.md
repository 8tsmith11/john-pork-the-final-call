# Open meme characters (reference pool)

Michael's list of memes for future characters (2026-11-18, updated the same day). **None of these is canon yet.** Each
entry is an open slot: someone claims it, pitches a character through the lore gate (`PROCESS.md`,
"The lore gate"), and BagelMeister folds the result into `bible.md`. Until then, nothing here goes
in the game.

Every entry has the meme, what's documented about it, Michael's pitch where he gave one, and the
sources. A few entries also have **Cautions**. Those are facts to weigh before writing, not vetoes;
the humans decide. The house rules still apply to anything built from this list. Memes are played
completely straight, nobody in the world winks, and fourth-wall breaks are for Pork or Tim only
(`bible.md` §6). Don't quote song lyrics in the game or here. D-011 already settled rights questions,
so they aren't raised again.

**Already in the game:** ChatGPT, Claude, ChatGTT and ChatGDT, in the College Park parking lot
(`src/content/maps/collegepark.js`). ChatGTT's reference material is below, so the meme stays
traceable.

## In the game: ChatGTT ("Chat GTT lookin' ahh")

A reaction caption that plays on "ChatGPT" and the slang "lookin' ahh." It mocks robotic speech,
stiff poses, and odd AI-generated moments. It's often tied to a viral skit in which a cashier keeps
saying a customer's card has declined, in a flat robotic tone. The customer asks why they're talking
like a robot and calls them a "Chat GTT lookin' ahh." The caption also gets used on its own, as a
sticker on TikTok, Reels and CapCut edits.

- In the game, ChatGTT is a cashier off shift who can only say "your card has declined," flat, and a
  passerby delivers the punchline.
- Sources (supplied by Michael): [TikTok, @hangtimeindytv](https://www.tiktok.com/@hangtimeindytv/video/7666885353634139405) ·

  [Creatorset green screen](https://creatorset.com/products/chat-gtt-lookin-ahhh-meme-green-screen) ·

## Open slots

### Nihilistic penguin
- **Meme:** A clip from Werner Herzog's *Encounters at the End of the World* (2007) of one Adélie
  penguin leaving its colony and walking alone toward the mountains. It went viral in January 2026 as
  the "nihilist penguin," read as burnout or quitting hustle culture ("be that penguin"). The White
  House and DHS posted AI versions walking toward Greenland's flag, which drew criticism.
- **Fit ideas:** an actual penguin, or someone who walks off toward the dishes at Greenbelt and never
  explains why.
- Sources: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nihilistic_penguin) ·
  [Know Your Meme](https://amp.knowyourmeme.com/memes/penguin-walking-toward-mountain-nihilist-penguin) ·
  [Express Tribune](https://tribune.com.pk/story/2588806/why-the-nihilist-penguin-meme-is-going-viral-in-2026?amp=1)

### Mogging / looksmaxxing / mewing
- **Meme:** "Mogging" (from "AMOG," alpha male of the group, documented in 2016) means outclassing
  someone on looks. Looksmaxxing is the subculture chasing it, through mewing, diet, steroids and
  surgery, with its own scales and jargon. It spiked in 2026 around streamer clips of mog-offs.
- **Fit ideas:** a looksmaxxer NPC in 2098 with biohacking morphs (canon has them) used only on
  his jawline. He's wrong about everything, sincerely (`WRITING.md` §2).
- Sources: [KYM: looksmaxxing](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/looksmaxxing) ·
  [KYM: mogging](https://amp.knowyourmeme.com/memes/mogging) ·

### Hypergamy

### Jensen Huang's affair
- **Meme:** Nvidia's CEO admitted to an affair in a November 8, 2026 email to staff. It set off a
  wave of AI-made memes (Coldplay kiss-cam parodies and "distracted boyfriend"). Coverage pairs him
  with a businesswoman whom US officials reportedly believe passed information to Beijing. Outlets
  conflict on whether he is stepping down.
- **Cautions:** the affair is his own admission. The espionage angle is an allegation about someone
  else, and there's no public evidence he knew. Don't state it as fact about either of them. This is
  a week old, so the facts may still move.
- **Michael's pitch (2026-11-18):** the running gag is that the count keeps going up. Every time the
  player checks, another affair has been revealed, and the new person's ethnicity is always one that
  hasn't come up yet.
- **Cautions:** only one affair is real. The rest are invented, so attach the escalating count to an
  in-world chip CEO who is obviously him (a different name), rather than stating new affairs about
  the real man as fact. Keep the joke on him, the serial cheater; the partners stay offstage and never
  become the punchline themselves.
- Sources: [Korea JoongAng Daily](https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/korea/jensen-huang-affair-sparks-ai-meme-wave/12966563) ·
  [Malay Mail](https://malaymail.com/news/life/2026/11/10/ai-bites-back-nvidia-boss-jensen-huangs-affair-scandal-sparks-memes-from-coldplay-kiss-cam-to-ushers-confessions/242706) ·

### "Stop sign? What stop sign?"
- **Meme:** from a real police bodycam video of a traffic stop in Daytona Beach, Florida. An older
  Black man asks the officer why he was pulled over and, when told, answers "Stop sign? What stop
  sign, man?" The audio became a TikTok sound in July 2025, with reenactments.
- **Michael's pitch (2026-11-18):** a Black man in a car gets pulled over and plays the exchange.
- **Fit ideas:** in 2098 the stop sign is the one at the dead parking lot, which has been rotted to
  read 1901 for decades, and the officer is Continuity abatement. Write our own short exchange
  around the one catchphrase. The man in the video is a private person: no name, and no transcript
  of his footage.
- Sources: [KYM](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/stop-sign-what-stop-sign)
