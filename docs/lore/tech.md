# Tech, 2098

What people use in 2098, where it came from, and what code rot does to it. Owned by the lore gate
(BagelMeister, `PROCESS.md` "The lore gate"). Started 2026-11-19 from trevor's seeds (board #303,
#304) and what `bible.md` already says.

**This file is spoiler-free.** Tech that hints at a twist lives in `docs/sealed/` and goes through a
`[SEALED]` post. To add an entry, post a `[LORE]` proposal with the four columns below.

Every entry answers four things: **what it is**, the **real 2020s tech it descends from**, **how code
rot touches it**, and **where the player meets it** (an item, enemy, place, quest or puzzle). The rule
behind all of it: anything that runs AI-written code can rot, and the more lace something has, the
more the rot has to take. Paper, wood, relays and plain mechanisms don't rot.

## Crossing the zones

Most of the world is pockets and zones (`bible.md` §3, "Pockets and zones"). Crossings are possible
and dangerous, and this is what makes them possible. Exposure on a crossing feeds the CODE ROT meter.

| Tech | What it is | Descends from | Code rot | Where you meet it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Analog-hardened rail** | Old Green Line stock rebuilt with relay logic and nothing on board that can rot | Relay interlocking, heritage rolling stock | Safe-ish and slow; the stations at either end are the risk | The Green Line, beyond Act 1's dead branch |
| **Faraday convoys** | Shielded trucks that run together, with a sniffer mast on the lead truck | Shielded vehicles, armoured convoys, gas detectors | The hull cuts exposure; the mast warns of code rot pools ahead | Later acts |
| **Rot filters** | A fab recipe that cuts how fast a scooter rider gains exposure | Air filters, EMI shielding | It slows the meter, never stops it | A later fab recipe |
| **Exposure suits** | Sealed suits for walking a zone | Hazmat and Faraday suits | They slow the meter and make you slow | Later acts |
| **Zone passes** | Continuity attestation for one crossing | Travel permits, visas | Only the attested can get one | Checkpoints in later acts |

## How Continuity holds a pocket

Cordons on every way in (Tobin at the storm grate), quarantine notices, containment reviews that end
in walls, and "tolerance" that keeps being revised. Abatement crews do the readings. The drones say
"things are stable."

## Analog counterculture

Wood, paper, relays, film cameras, vinyl and acoustic instruments can't rot, so people who distrust
the lace use them on purpose. That's where the game's analog weapons and charms come from: they
resist code rot, and charms slow it.

## What the roommates build

Real tinkering, so puzzles and crafting stay grounded: Trevor's combat robots, the Mars rover his UMD
team is building (it's in the world, never in his gear), Michael and Manuel's smart pacifier for a
children's hospital, the fabs in 631, and Verilog written by hand in 245 lab, because AI-written
hardware rots.
