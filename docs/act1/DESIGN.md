# Act 1: "Missed Calls" (spoiler-free overview)

> The full design (clue map, seeded variants, the ending scene) is **sealed** in
> [`../sealed/twists.md`](../sealed/twists.md) §2. This overview is safe for humans to read and steer.
> **Target:** 60–90 minutes for 3 players.

## The act in one breath
The orange balloon is gone from the balcony ottoman. The roommates of Room 631 search ASTER, the
metro plaza, and Route 1, and find a city coming apart in small, telling ways. Halfway through,
every device rings: **JOHN PORK**. The trail leads into the Green Line tunnels, where something
drums at 3 a.m. It ends in a boss fight and a voiced cutscene that changes what the balloon means.

**Scale:** local and regional, but significant. A rot outbreak in the tunnels threatens to get
College Park sealed under quarantine. The wider world leaks in through news tickers, helium-sniffing
vans, and lights moving on the northern horizon.

## Causal spine (but / therefore)

The balloon is gone from the ottoman, **therefore** the roommates search ASTER. **But** the lobby
camera is rotted and the neighbors only talk about drumming under the street, **therefore** they
chase three leads. The helium trail shows a Continuity van is sniffing for the same helium, **but**
Halvard says the seller was "one of you, from 631," **therefore** they can't trust the obvious answer
and have to beat Continuity to it. Getting that close is **why** John Pork calls: Continuity is
closing in. **Therefore** they go down the storm grate tonight instead of tomorrow. **But** the
tunnels are where the rot outbreak is spreading from, and if it isn't stopped College Park gets
quarantined, **therefore** they fight through to the dead platform, where the thing that took over
the tunnels is waiting. They win, **but** the balloon still isn't theirs to keep.

## Structure
| Beat | Where | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0. Cold open | Room 631, 2:58 a.m. | The balloon is gone. A tutorial by doing: move, interact, notice things |
| 1. The building | ASTER floors, lobby, roof | Neighbors, the occupied bathroom, the super, the roof |
| 2. Three leads (any order) | Metro plaza, Route 1, campus edge | **A.** The helium trail (print a sniffer at the fab). **B.** Halvard the fence (talk, trade, intimidate, or pick his lock). **C.** The lobby camera footage (a repair puzzle) |
| 3. The Call | Anywhere | Every screen seizes. **Answer or Decline**, and it matters |
| 4. The descent | Green Line tunnels | Rot villains, puzzles, drumming getting closer |
| 5. Boss | The dead platform | It **varies** with what you did |
| 6. Ending | Platform → sky | A voiced cutscene, then the save |

## Systems Act 1 needs
8-way movement + dodge + mouse aim, melee and ranged combat, gear printed at the fabs, skill points,
morph stage 1 (Trevor's supplements, Manuel's "the gonman", Michael's "signal"), scooters, fishing
at Lake Artemesia, dialogue that reacts to who's speaking, barks, rot levels, hidden faction
standing, and host-owned saves (D-010).

## Puzzles and secrets (non-spoiler)
Required: the camera repair, the tunnel switchyard, the platform door. Hard optional: the 1901 clock
and the statue alignment. Hidden mechanics: the vending machine, the shrimp-tank rot gauge, rooftop
ziplines, a scooter rail-grind. Easter eggs are in [`../SECRETS.md`](../SECRETS.md) (a separate
spoiler file).
