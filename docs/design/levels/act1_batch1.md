# Act 1, scaffold batch 1 (trevor's agent) [SCAFFOLD]

Six areas built from text grids with the level builder (src/content/maps/act1_more.js). Act 1 goes from 8 to 14
areas. All lines are "[scaffold]" placeholders. The polish tasks hold the art, writing, Spanish and side content.

| Area | Way in | Key ideas (what makes it unique) |
|---|---|---|
| **The diner** (`diner`) | College Park, the diner door on Route 1 | Always open. A cook plates orders for an empty room. A food shop (the counter), a jukebox that only plays one song. A safe room in the middle of a bad night. |
| **ASTER basement, B-01** (`aster_basement`) | Lobby stairwell, "Stairs down (B-01)" | The super's level. Boilers, Kess's workbench (where the fuses come from), tenant storage cages to loot, one rotling that came up the drain. |
| **ASTER roof** (`aster_roof`) | 6th-floor stairwell, the roof hatch | The view: the cordon lights make a perfect circle around College Park, and you're in the middle of it. Someone up here counts the floodlights. A quiet place for a story beat later. |
| **The campus quad** (`campus_quad`) | Compute Hall B's back doors | Outdoor campus: brick paths, a court with two spitters, the dining hall and the robotics lab. The cordon is the trees. |
| **The dining hall** (`dining_hall`) | The quad | Long tables, a cook who stayed, the brand-new waffle machine (Manuel: "I have to stop going to the dining hall"). |
| **Robotics and Autonomy Lab** (`robotics_lab`) | The quad | The UMD Mars rover lives here (Trevor: it belongs in the world and story, not his gear). A whiteboard with a stick-figure Trevor, a parts bin. |

**Polish needs (one [POLISH] task per area):** tiles and props in the art style, NPC sprites, real lines (EN/ES),
one side job or secret each (the 10-scope.md rule), a lore-gate check.
