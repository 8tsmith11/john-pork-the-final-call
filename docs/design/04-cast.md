## How a character is written

Each named character gets six things, and a line of dialogue should be traceable to at least one.

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Grade** | Baseline, Laced, Edited, Rebuilt, or Shell (a machine in a grown or built body) |
| **Wants** | What they would ask for if asked |
| **Voice** | One habit of speech concrete enough to recognize with the name covered |
| **Found** | Where and when. Most people keep hours |
| **Gives** | The quest, service or information the party gets from them |
| **Unexpected** | One thing the player can do that this person reacts to and no one else does |

**Voice rules for the whole cast.** People talk about the wrong thing when it matters. Nobody
explains their own feelings. Half the cast is bad at talking and that is how you tell them apart:
one never finishes a sentence, one only asks questions, one answers a different question from the
one asked. Nobody is as articulate as a writer.

### Manuelita

Michael's girlfriend (renamed from Nari, D-014). **Off screen until Michael is reunited with her**; after that she walks with
the party and buffs everyone near her (`src/content/romance.js`). Until then she is present as a photo turned face-down, a sweatshirt that
isn't his size, a contact that goes to voicemail, and the fact that he brings her up in every
region as if she'll want to hear about it.

- **Who she is:** Manuelita Seo. A sound engineer who restored old recordings for a living: tape,
  wax, wire. She could tell you what room a voice was recorded in.
- **What Michael knows:** eight months ago she took a call, listened for a long time without
  speaking, wrote something on her hand, and left for work. Her badge logged in. Nobody saw her
  come out.
- **What he does about it:** talks to her voicemail. Keeps her last message unplayed so there is
  still one left.
- **How it plays:** his barks about her are funny for the first hour and stop being funny one at a
  time. His arc is about answering a ringing phone. It is not about giving her up.
- 🔒 What she heard, and where she is.

## Casting rules

1. **Nobody is a strawman.** Each named character can make their case in a way a reasonable
   player might accept.
2. **Schedules.** People are somewhere at a time. Coming back at a different hour finds a
   different conversation.
3. **Every act adds, none introduces everyone.** Act 1 uses ring one only, plus voices of the
   absent (Vance on a notice, the Director on a screen, Solomon on a pirate broadcast).
4. **Three reactions each.** Every named character reacts differently to each roommate, at least
   once.
5. **Shells are people.** No shell in this cast is written as a device.
6. **Names.** The cast is as mixed as Maryland is. No name is a joke.
